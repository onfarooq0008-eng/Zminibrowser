About Veya Browser

Developed by Umar Farooq

Veya Browser is a free web browser for Android, built to be quick and light on your phone's storage and battery.

Features

  • Fast browsing with multiple tabs and private (incognito) tabs
  • Built-in ad and tracker blocker, pop-up blocking and cookie controls
  • Download manager with a fast option and a slower, ad-free option
  • Built-in video player with playback speed and picture-in-picture
  • Bookmarks, history, home-page shortcuts and QR code scanner
  • Light and dark themes, desktop-site mode and custom user agents

How the app is funded

Veya Browser is free. To cover development costs, the app shows full-screen (interstitial) ads from Meta Audience Network when you open or close the app, and when you choose the fast download option. The slow download option never shows an ad. See the Privacy Policy for details.
