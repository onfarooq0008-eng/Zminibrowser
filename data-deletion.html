Data Deletion

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Veya Browser has no user accounts and we do not store personal data about you on our own servers. That means there is no account for us to delete. Here is how to remove the data that does exist.

Data on your device

  1. Open Veya Browser and go to Menu → Clear Browsing Data.
  2. Select what to erase (history, cookies, cache, site data, downloads history, form data, saved permissions) and confirm.
  3. To remove everything the app stored, uninstall the app, or on Android go to Settings → Apps → Veya Browser → Storage → Clear data.

Files you downloaded are saved in your device’s Downloads folder. Delete them with your file manager.

Advertising data

Ads are provided by Meta Audience Network, which handles ad-related data under Meta’s Privacy Policy. To limit it, reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads), and use the data controls in your Meta account if you have one.
