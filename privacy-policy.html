Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Veya Browser (“the app”), developed by Umar Farooq (“we”, “us”), handles information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Summary
  • No account is needed and we do not ask for your name, email or phone number inside the app.
  • Your bookmarks, history and settings stay on your device. We do not receive them.
  • The app shows ads from Meta Audience Network. Meta may collect device information, such as your advertising ID and IP address, to deliver and measure ads.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

1. Information stored on your device

To work as a browser, the app stores the following locally on your device: bookmarks, home-page shortcuts, browsing history (not saved in private tabs), download history, per-site settings, app settings, cookies and website data kept by Android WebView, and a counter of requests blocked by the ad blocker. We do not receive or have access to this data. You can erase it any time from Menu → Clear Browsing Data, or by uninstalling the app.

2. Information we collect

We do not run our own servers for the app and we do not collect personal information ourselves. We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. The only third-party SDK that communicates with its own servers is the advertising SDK described below.

3. Advertising (Meta Audience Network)

The app uses the Meta Audience Network SDK to show interstitial (full-screen) ads. These ads can appear when you open the app, when you close the app, and when you choose the “fast download” option. To load, show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the SDK may collect and process information from your device, which can include:

  • Advertising ID (such as the Google Advertising ID) and device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and OS details (model, OS version, language, screen size, network type)
  • Ad interaction data (ads shown, clicked or dismissed)

This information is processed by Meta under Meta’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive your personal data from Meta; we only receive aggregated advertising performance reports.

Your ad choices. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your device settings (on most Android devices: Settings → Privacy → Ads). You can also manage ad preferences in your Meta account’s ad settings. Opting out does not remove ads; it makes them less personalized.

4. Websites you visit

When you open a website, your device connects directly to that site and to the third-party content it loads. That site receives standard information such as your IP address and browser details, and may set cookies under its own privacy policy. We do not see or control this. The app offers tools that can reduce tracking: an ad and tracker blocker, third-party cookie blocking, an optional Do Not Track request, private tabs and a clear-data-on-exit option. Private tabs do not save history, but they do not make you anonymous to websites, your network or your internet provider. The built-in ad blocker applies to web pages only and does not block the app’s own ads.

5. Search

Text you type in the address bar that is not a web address is sent to the search engine you select in Settings (Google by default, or Bing, DuckDuckGo, Yahoo or a custom one). That search engine’s privacy policy applies.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only (a) with Meta Audience Network as described above, and (b) if required by law.

8. Data retention and your rights

We do not hold personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side. Data on your device is under your control. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California) you may have rights over data held by Meta, such as access, deletion or opting out of personalized ads; these are exercised with Meta and through your device settings. For anything related to the app, contact us at admin@fastvpnn.pp.ua. See also Data Deletion.

9. Children

The app is a general-purpose browser and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. Because it provides open access to the internet, parents should supervise use by minors. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help.

10. Security

The app blocks pages with invalid security certificates and does not expose device features to websites. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page with its date above. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.
