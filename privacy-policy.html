Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Veya Browser (“the app”), developed by Umar Farooq (“we”, “us”), handles information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Summary
  • No account is needed and we do not ask for your name, email or phone number inside the app.
  • Your bookmarks, history, downloads list and settings stay on your device. We do not receive them.
  • The app shows ads from Meta Audience Network when you close the app and when you start a download. Meta may collect device information, such as your advertising ID and IP address, to deliver and measure ads.
  • To block ads, the app downloads public filter lists (EasyList and EasyPrivacy) from the internet every few days.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

1. Information stored on your device

To work as a browser, the app stores the following locally on your device: bookmarks, home-page shortcuts, browsing history (not saved in private tabs), the list of your downloads, per-site settings, app settings, cookies and website data kept by Android WebView, a counter of blocked requests, your user scripts, the elements you hid with “Mark as ad”, and (only if you turn it on) the addresses of your open tabs so they can be restored. We do not receive or have access to this data. You can erase it from Menu → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app.

2. Information we collect

We do not run our own servers for the app and we do not collect personal information ourselves. We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. The third-party services the app talks to are described below.

3. Advertising (Meta Audience Network)

The app uses the Meta Audience Network SDK to show interstitial (full-screen) ads. These ads can appear when you close the app and when you start a download. To load, show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the SDK may collect and process information from your device, which can include:

  • Advertising ID (such as the Google Advertising ID) and device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and OS details (model, OS version, language, screen size, network type)
  • Ad interaction data (ads shown, clicked or dismissed)

This information is processed by Meta under Meta’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive your personal data from Meta; we only receive aggregated advertising performance reports.

Your ad choices. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your device settings (on most Android devices: Settings → Privacy → Ads). Opting out does not remove ads; it makes them less personalized.

4. Ad blocker filter lists

The ad blocker uses the public EasyList and EasyPrivacy filter lists. The app downloads them from easylist.to (or the mirror easylist-downloads.adblockplus.org) when you first use it and then about every 4 days. You can turn the automatic update off, or update manually, in Settings → General → Ad blocking. Those servers can see your IP address and that a download was requested, like any website. No browsing data is sent.

5. Downloads

When you download a file, the app connects directly to the website that hosts it, sending the cookies and the address of the page you came from (so the site allows the download), the same way a browser does. Files are saved in the “Veya Browser” folder inside your phone’s Downloads folder. Downloads run in the background and show a notification with their progress. We do not upload or scan your files.

6. Websites you visit

When you open a website, your device connects directly to that site and to the third-party content it loads. That site receives standard information such as your IP address and browser details, and may set cookies under its own privacy policy. We do not see or control this. The app offers tools that can reduce tracking: an ad and tracker blocker, third-party cookie blocking, optional Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control requests, private tabs and a clear-data-on-exit option. Private tabs do not save history, but they do not make you anonymous to websites, your network or your internet provider.

7. Search, translate and AI

Text you type in the address bar that is not a web address is sent to the search engine you select in Settings (Google by default, or Bing, DuckDuckGo, Yahoo or a custom one). The Translate feature opens the page through Google Translate. The AI feature only works when you tap it: it copies the page text and your request to your clipboard and opens the AI website you chose in Settings, where you paste it yourself. That website’s privacy policy applies to what you paste.

8. Links that open in other apps

When a link can be opened by another app you have installed (for example YouTube), the app asks you whether to open it there. To do this it checks which installed apps handle that kind of link; this check stays on your device and is not collected. If you choose to open the app, the link is passed to that app.

10. Default browser reminder

If Veya Browser is not your default browser, the app may show a reminder at most once every 4 days. You can choose “Don’t ask again”. Nothing is sent anywhere when you answer.

11. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only (a) with Meta Audience Network as described above, and (b) if required by law.

12. Data retention and your rights

We do not hold personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side. Data on your device is under your control. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California) you may have rights over data held by Meta, such as access, deletion or opting out of personalized ads; these are exercised with Meta and through your device settings. For anything related to the app, contact us at admin@fastvpnn.pp.ua. See also Data Deletion.

13. Children

The app is a general-purpose browser and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. Because it provides open access to the internet, parents should supervise use by minors. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help.

14. Security

The app blocks pages with invalid security certificates unless you choose to continue, and does not expose device features to websites. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page with its date above. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.
